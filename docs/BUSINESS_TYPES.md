# BUSINESS_TYPES.md: Presets

Status: v1.0 (7 Nov 2026). A preset only sets defaults (modules, wording, roles, template). No preset has its own code.

Launch presets: **Hotel/Lodge** and **Restaurant & Bar** (D-016). Other types postponed until a paying client asks.

## 1. Hotel/Lodge
- **Main pain:** not found on Google Maps; bookings by phone and platform listings (TripAdvisor, Booking.com); relies on regulars.
- **Pitch:** "Be found on Google Maps and get booked directly." We add direct bookings alongside platform listings, we don't replace them.
- **Starter:** website, room list with prices (RWF and USD), Call and WhatsApp buttons, Google Maps / Business Profile setup, basic SEO checklist, basic phone photos, click stats.
- **Typical add-ons:** booking requests, booking calendar, repeat guest list, restaurant modules (off by default), guest requests by QR in room, housekeeping, check-in, staff, stock, reports, professional photo shoot.
- **Wording:** room, room type, guest, booking, stay, check-in.
- **Default roles:** owner (P1), manager (P2), receptionist and housekeeping (P3). Restaurant roles only if restaurant modules are on.
- **Default template:** warm lodge.

## 2. Restaurant & Bar
- **Main pain:** orders, waiting, staff, stock, daily cash. Bars here also run kitchens.
- **Pitch:** "We make logistics easy. No client waits."
- **Venue style setting:** restaurant-first or bar-first. Changes wording and default template only. A bar without food leaves the kitchen role off.
- **Starter:** website, menu or drinks list with prices (RWF and USD), Call and WhatsApp buttons, Google Maps / Business Profile setup, basic SEO checklist, basic phone photos, click stats.
- **Typical add-ons:** menu + WhatsApp orders (table QR, web, order-ahead pickup), QR table requests (call waiter, request bill), manager orders page, staff order flow, staff management, stock, sales and reports, customer list, professional photo shoot.
- **Wording:** restaurant-first: table, order, dish, bill. Bar-first: table or counter, tab, drink.
- **Default roles:** owner (P1), manager (P2), waiter, kitchen, bar (P3).
- **Default template:** modern/minimal (restaurant-first), bold safari (bar-first).

## 3. Hotel with restaurant or bar
One business, Hotel/Lodge preset, with restaurant modules switched on (D-009). Same login, same staff list.

## 4. Postponed types
Tour and trekking operators, guesthouses (may fit Hotel/Lodge), shops, salons, car rentals. Added only when a paying client asks; each must reuse existing modules where possible.
