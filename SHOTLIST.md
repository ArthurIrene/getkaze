## Shrinking (target under ~150 KB each)
- Easiest: squoosh.app → WebP, quality ~75, resize to the width above.
- Or on Ubuntu: `sudo apt install webp` then `cwebp -q 75 -resize 1000 0 input.jpg -o output.webp`
